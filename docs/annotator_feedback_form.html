1. Purpose of this test

The Glow app uses an on-device AI model to analyze a user's selfie and detect their face shape, eye shape, skin tone, and undertone, then recommends a makeup look based on those results. This test asks a professional makeup artist to independently evaluate the same participants, so we can measure:

  1. Detection accuracy — do the AI's face/eye-shape and skin-tone/undertone readings match what a trained expert would conclude by eye?
  2. Recommendation appropriateness — does the makeup look the app suggests actually suit that person, in the professional judgment of the makeup artist?

This test is being run against the ISO/IEC 25010 software quality model, under the Functional Suitability characteristic — specifically its Functional Correctness (are the results right?) and Functional Appropriateness (do the results actually help the user accomplish their goal?) sub-characteristics.

2. How to fill out each sheet

There is one score sheet per participant (20 total). For each participant, please follow these steps in order — this order matters, so that your judgment is recorded before you see the app's answer, not influenced by it:

  1. The participant completes the in-app face scan.
  2. Before the app's result is shown to you, examine the participant in person and fill in Step 1 on the sheet: your own independent read of their face shape, eye shape, skin tone, and undertone, plus the makeup look you would personally recommend for them.
  3. Now reveal the app's result screen and copy its output into Step 2.
  4. Compare your answers to the app's in Step 3, marking each attribute Match / Partial / No Match, with a short note on anything that surprised you.
  5. Look at the makeup look the app recommended and rate it in Step 4 — would it actually suit this person, in your professional opinion?

3. Rating legend

Match levelMeaning
MatchYour independent read and the app's result are the same category.
PartialClose / borderline / adjacent category (e.g. you said Oval, app said Long).
No MatchClearly different categories.
Suitability scoreMeaning
1Strongly disagree / not suitable at all
2Disagree / mostly not suitable
3Neutral / acceptable but not ideal
4Agree / suitable
5Strongly agree / very suitable, would use as-is on a client

Note on lighting: skin tone and undertone perception change with lighting. Please note the lighting condition for each session, and try to keep it consistent across participants where possible.
